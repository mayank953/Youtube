§0 Summary

I'm a Tech Associate at Goldman Sachs in the Controllers Department. I build the refiners and pipelines that turn hundreds of millions of trades and billions in transfers into the numbers a bank reports — to its regulators and to its own books.

Six years of that work: Spark refiners handling 150M+ trades, transfer-pricing automation across GSCO entities, and confidential position sourcing — systems that have to be correct, on time, and auditable. Before Goldman Sachs I shipped machine-learning models at OYO Rooms and researched ensemble methods at IIIT-Delhi.
